Project references

Attributions

Sources for visual assets, map data, and route information used on this site.

Map and place data

  • Map tiles and community map data: OpenStreetMap contributors, © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Place search and reverse geocoding use Nominatim. Searches are sent only when a visitor submits a place or selects a map location.
  • Transit feed stop listings, when available, come from Transitland. A visitor-provided restricted API key is required; requests are made directly from the browser.

Images

  • Kampala public transport route map: map artwork and institutional marks appear in the supplied image. See Kampala Capital City Authority and UN-Habitat.
  • Project-supplied image files: express-route.jpg, peak-transit.jpg, taxi-routes.webp, favicon.png, and og-image.jpg. The original creator and license details were not included with these files; add them before publishing if the images require attribution.

Route information

Route names, stage locations, schedule windows, journey times, and fare ranges are illustrative student-project estimates. Stage map positions are approximate search matches. They are not an official schedule, fare, or stop database; confirm details locally.
